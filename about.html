About PulsePoint

PulsePoint is a student-focused public health survey web prototype designed for a first-year college computer science assignment. It enables anonymous data collection on sleep, hydration, and academic stress, then visualizes community trends in real-time.

Project Information

Project Name: PulsePoint (Public Health Survey Platform)
Technology: HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JavaScript (No external frameworks)
Data Storage: Browser window.localStorage
Visualization: Dynamic CSS Bar Charts (Responsive & Lightweight)
Project Scope: Non-clinical educational demonstration

How the System Works (Viva Explanation)

  1. The Survey Form (survey.html): Uses standard HTML radio inputs with a progress bar that recalculates whenever an option is selected.
  2. Validation & Storage (survey.js): When submitted, it prevents page reload with event.preventDefault(), checks that all questions are answered, and stores the response in localStorage as a JSON string.
  3. Personal Results Alone (dashboard.html): Reads pulsepoint_my_last_submission and presents your individual answers and customized health insights in a dedicated top profile card.
  4. Analytics Engine (dashboard.js): Reads all responses from pulsepoint_simple_data, calculates totals and percentages, updates summary cards, and dynamically animates the CSS bar charts by adjusting their style.width.

Privacy & Ethics Notice

This prototype strictly enforces participant anonymity. No names, student roll numbers, email addresses, or IP addresses are recorded. All survey data stays on your personal device inside your browser's local storage.
